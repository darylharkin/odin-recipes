Poached Eggs

Description

It's poached eggs, mate.

Think of it as a boiled egg without the helmet.

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp of vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp of salt
  • 2 large fresh eggs

Steps

  1. Add vinegar to a saucepan of water and bring to the boil
  2. crack the eggs into a bowl
  3. stir the boiling water so that it's spinning around the saucepan and pour the eggs into the water
  4. Let heat for one or two minutes
  5. Serve on toast with salt and pepper
