Halloumi Fries

Description

This is one of my favourite, simplest comfort foods, introduced to me by the catering team on a tour a few years back.

Every time I have this dish, I'm reminded of the tour. I pick up a packet of halloumi every time I'm in the supermarket.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet of halloumi
  • Paprika
  • Olive oil spray

Steps

  1. Open up the packet of halloumi and dry it off with some kitchen towel
  2. Cut the halloumi into chip sized slices
  3. Spray with olive oil, making sure each fry is coated on all sides
  4. Dust with paprika, shaking the fries to make sure the paprika covers most if not all of the fry
  5. Airfry for 15 mins at 180, making sure to give the basket a shake every 5 mins.
  6. Serve with your dipping sauce of choice
