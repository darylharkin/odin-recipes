Grilled Cheese

Description

Everyone needs to know how to make this classic comfort food

Cheap, easy to make and delicious

Ingredients

  • White bread
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Paprika

Steps

  1. Butter one slice and place butter side down on a hot pan
  2. Add cheese and Parika to the bread
  3. Place another slice of buttered bread on top, butter side up
  4. Cook until bottom slice is browned and then flip
  5. Do the same on the other side
  6. Serve with some salt
